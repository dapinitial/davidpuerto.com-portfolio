Privacy

Effective October 19, 2026

This is my personal site and portfolio. There are no accounts, no ads, and nothing is sold. Here is everything it collects, and why.

Analytics

I use Google Analytics to see which pages get read, roughly where visitors are (city level), what device and browser they use, and how they found the site. It sets first-party cookies (_ga) to tell a return visit from a new one. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses, and I can't see who you are.

If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, the analytics script never loads. A content blocker works too, and the site behaves the same either way.

The contact form

When you send a message, your name, email address and message are emailed to me through Resend, an email delivery service. The site itself does not keep a copy. I use your email only to reply.

The password-protected case studies

Some case studies are under NDA and sit behind a password. When you sign in, the site sets one cookie, cs_auth, so you stay signed in for up to 30 days. It holds no personal information, only proof that the password was entered. Sign-in attempts are counted per network address for a few minutes to stop password guessing, and those counts are kept in memory, never written down.

Things your browser keeps

The site stores two small values in your browser's session storage so the page transitions and loader animation play once. They are never sent anywhere and disappear when you close the tab.

Embedded media

A few case studies embed videos or demos from YouTube (in its privacy-enhanced mode), CodePen or Dropbox. Those services may set their own cookies when you play the embed, under their own privacy policies.

Hosting

The site runs on DigitalOcean, which handles every request the way any web host does. I don't keep server logs of visits.

Your choices

You can block or clear cookies at any time. If you've emailed me and want that correspondence deleted, or want to know what I have, write to me@davidpuerto.com and I'll take care of it.

Changes

If this changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
